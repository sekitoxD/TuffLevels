# Notable Horde quests and chains (1-60)

See [README.md](README.md) for the legend, source abbreviations and confidence caveats. Research
only: nothing here is loaded by the addon or turned into steps yet. Per Plan 3 a human reads this,
decides, and writes the step.

This file lists the quests and chains that leveling guides repeat as "big ticket": high XP for the
time, hubs worth clearing, chains that pay off later, and class chains that change how fast a
character levels. Weapon and gear rewards per class live in `../classes/`; this file adds the
**quest-chain detail** for the ones that matter and the general (all-class) chains.

Race-locked entries are marked (Orc/Troll, Tauren, Undead). Classic Era has no Horde Paladin and no
Blood Elves, so Eversong/Ghostlands are out.

## Levels 1-30

### Starter zones
- **Durotar chains (Orc/Troll), L1-10, 3 srcs (WT, WH, JW).** Standard ramp: Your Place In The World,
  Sarkoth, Sen'jin batch (A Solvent Spirit, Practical Prey, Zalazane, Minshina's Skull, Thwarting
  Kolkar Aggression), Razor Hill batch (Dark Storms, Encroachment, Break a Few Eggs, Vanquish the
  Betrayers, Carry Your Weight, The Admiral's Orders). Carry Your Weight gives a bag; Skull Rock
  gives a first Uncommon. WT: Echo Isles is quicker to leave by dying and releasing.
- **Skull Rock chain into the Barrens, L9-12, 2 srcs (WT, JW).** Dark Storms, Margoz, Skull Rock,
  Neeru Fireblade, Ak'Zeloth, The Demon Seed; plus Conscript of the Horde, then Crossroads
  Conscription. Natural handoff to the Barrens; WT sets the hearth at the Crossroads here. Skip The
  Demon Seed when the rare elite Rathorian is at the altar (2 srcs).
- **Hidden Enemies chain, L9+, 4 srcs (WT, ZK, IV, JW).** Five quests from Thrall, ending in a choice of
  Kris, Hammer, Axe or Staff of Orgrimmar. The last leg is a **Ragefire Chasm** kill (1,150 XP). WT
  calls it "notoriously frustrating"; JW says skip the dungeon part. Only worth it if RFC is on the
  route.
- **Mulgore (Tauren) / Tirisfal (Undead), 4 srcs.** WIC calls Mulgore the weakest starter and suggests
  crossing to Durotar at 9 to finish out to 12. Undead: Tirisfal 1-10, Silverpine 10-20, though
  Silverpine quests thin out around 15 and WIC/WH call it weaker than the Barrens. WT starts both
  races at the Crossroads at 12.

### The Barrens (12-26)
- **Crossroads opening batch, L12-16, 4 srcs (WT, WIC, WH, JW).** Plainstrider Menace, The Zhevra,
  Raptor Thieves, Supplies for the Crossroads, The Angry Scytheclaws, Fungal Spores, The Forgotten
  Pools, Disrupt the Attacks, Harpy Raiders. WIC: "you can earn 76,000 XP solely from completing
  these quests" (12-20 list). GR: one of the densest XP zones; stay into the early 20s.
- **Kolkar Leaders line, L12-16, 3 srcs (WT, WIC, WH).** Kolkar Leaders and Centaur Bracers, Verog the
  Dervish, Hezrul Bloodmark (first ring). WT: Stagnant Oasis is "one of the best places to grind for
  your level".
- **Ratchet cluster, L13-17, 3 srcs (WT, WIC, WH).** Wharfmaster Dizzywig, Southsea Freebooters, WANTED:
  Baron Longshore, The Guns of Northwatch (ends in an escort). Gets the Ratchet flight path and the
  boat to Booty Bay.
- **Samophlange chain, L14-20, 3 srcs (WT, WIC, WH).** Ratchet, four console quests north of the
  Crossroads, Wenikee Boltbucket, Rilli Greasygob (Orgrimmar), Samophlange Manual. WT: last turn-in
  "should get you to level 20". Reward: first Uncommon shield.
- **Forgotten Pools to Leaders of the Fang, L13-22, 4 srcs (WT, ZK, IV, WIC).** The Forgotten Pools
  (Tonga Runetotem), The Stagnant Oasis, Altered Beings, Hamuul Runetotem (Thunder Bluff), Nara
  Wildmane, Leaders of the Fang (Wailing Caverns; Crescent Staff or Wingblade). Chain is 5 or 6
  quests depending on the source. Solo until the dungeon step.
- **The Warsong Reports, L17-19, 1 src (WT).** A flight-path tour: Splintertree Post, Satyrnaar,
  Zoram'gar. Taken "primarily for the flight paths".
- **Southern Barrens rewards cluster, L25-27, 3 srcs (WT, WH, WP partial).** Egg Hunt, Betrayal from
  Within, Weapons of Choice, Gann's Reclamation then Revenge of Gann, Isha Awak, Washte Pawne. Reward
  rich; ends with the Booty Bay boat.

### Stonetalon and Ashenvale (17-28)
- **Stonetalon first pass, L17-18, 3 srcs.** Avenge My Village, Protect Kaya (escort, soloable), Blood
  Feeders. Sun Rock Retreat flight path. Trouble in the Deeps (440 XP) here is the prerequisite for
  the BFD quest The Essence of Aku'Mai.
- **Stonetalon second pass, L23-28, 4 srcs (WIC, WH, IV, GR).** WIC: at 23 go to Sun Rock Retreat and
  Malaka'jin, "you should reach level 25 very quickly". **WT skips Stonetalon between 18 and 30.** No
  guide read gave a full quest list for this range.
- **Cry of the Thunderhawk to The Ashenvale Hunt, L20-21, 3 srcs (WT, WP, WH).** The practical way to
  open Ashenvale and pick up two flight paths.
- **Zoram'gar Outpost cluster, L21-24, 3 srcs.** Naga at the Zoram Strand, Vorsha the Lasher (Muglash
  escort), Thistlefur, King of the Foulweald, Je'neu of the Earthen Ring. Skip the Thistlefur cave
  escort unless grouped.
- **Splintertree Post cluster, L24-27, 4 srcs (WT, WP, WH, NTB).** Satyr Horns, Warsong Supplies (can
  give a first Rare per WH), Torek's Assault (escort), Ursangous's Paw, Shadumbra's Head. Takes you
  from about 24 to 27. NTB says group for Shadumbra; WT/WP solo her.

### Hillsbrad (22-30)
- **Tarren Mill quest block, L22-30, 6 srcs (WT, WP, WH, WIC, IV, GR).** Entry from the Sepulcher
  (Journey to Hillsbrad Foothills, Beren's Peril). Durnholde: WANTED: Syndicate Personnel, The
  Rescue, Elixir of Suffering. Battle of Hillsbrad chain across farms, forge, City Hall and the
  mine. Elixir of Pain (Enraged Stanley "about 1100 xp" per WP), Elixir of Agony, Souvenirs of Death,
  Dangerous!.
- **Sequencing:** WT and WP both start about 22-23, leave at 24 with Souvenirs of Death half done, and
  return at 27-28. IV: Hillsbrad at 23-24 and 29-30. WIC offers Hillsbrad **or** Shadowfang Keep at
  25-27. WT and WP are probably one lineage (WP is a 2007 guide), so their agreement is one vote.
- Hillsbrad is a PvP zone; WH says group whenever possible.

### Thousand Needles (28-34)
- **Freewind Post hub, L28-31, 6 srcs.** Entry via Melor Sends Word and Message to Freewind Post;
  Pacify the Centaur, Alien Egg, Wanted - Arnak Grimtotem, Grimtotem Spying; Test of Faith then Test of
  Endurance; Homeward Bound (escort); Hypercapacitor Gizmo; Sacred Fire then Arikara; Free at Last.
  WIC: 36,800 raw XP for 27-30. Test of Endurance crate waves are very difficult solo.
- **Mirage Raceway / Shimmering Flats, L30-34, 7 srcs.** See levels 31-60.

## Levels 31-60

Level = where the sources place it; they drift by 2-4 levels. IV and WT route differently; both are
summarized under [Zone order](#zone-order-the-guides-use).

### 31-37
- **Test of Faith / Test of Endurance chain, TN L28-31, 4 srcs (WT, JW, IV, WH).** IV's full chain: Test
  of Faith, Test of Endurance, Test of Strength, Test of Lore (x6), Final Passage. Test of Lore is the
  **Scarlet Monastery Library** quest (2,100 XP per WT). Abandon Test of Endurance if you cannot solo
  the wave fight (JW, WT).
- **Arikara / Hypercapacitor Gizmo, then Steelsnap then Frostmaw, L29-37, 4 srcs.** Long chain across
  Thousand Needles, Thunder Bluff and Alterac. Frostmaw is turned in at Thunder Bluff around 35-37.
- **Mirage Raceway hub, L31-34, 7 srcs (WT, JW, WCL, WH, GR, WIC, IV).** Dizzywig, Hemet Nesingwary,
  Hardened Shells, Salt Flat Venom, Load Lightening, A Bump in the Road, Rocket Car Parts. "Complete
  every quest from this small hub just by doing circles around the raceway" (GR). Turn-ins hand you
  Goblin Sponsorship and Martek the Exiled (Martek becomes Indurium in the Badlands, then News for
  Fizzle around 45).
- **Nesingwary chains and The Green Hills of Stranglethorn, STV L31-45, 5 srcs.** Raptor, Tiger and
  Panther Mastery, each with follow-ups as you level; the top of each gets elite. Green Hills is four
  chapter quests turned in at 43 (WT); keep or buy the pages. **This is also the Big Game Hunter
  prerequisite** (see Hunter).
- **Grom'gol and Booty Bay kill chains, STV L33-44, 4 srcs (WT, JW, WCL, WH).** Grom'gol: Trollbane,
  Hunt for Yenniku, Bloody Bone Necklaces, The Vile Reef (elite), The Defense of Grom'gol, Headhunting,
  Split Bone Necklaces, Mok'thardin's Enchantment, Bloodscalp Clan Heads. Booty Bay: Singing Blue
  Shards, Investigate the Camp, Bloodscalp Ears then Skullsplitter Tusks, Hostile Takeover, Supply and
  Demand, Excelsior.
- **Hillsbrad / Alterac carryover, L34-36, 3 srcs.** Helcular's Revenge, The Hammer May Fall, Prison
  Break In, Elixir of Agony, Dalaran Patrols, Battle of Hillsbrad (best grouped). Battle of
  Hillsbrad's turn-in at Undercity also grants **Into the Scarlet Monastery**.
- **Hammerfall, Arathi L34-38, 5 srcs.** The Hammer May Fall then Call to Arms, Raising Spirits then Guile
  of the Raptor, Foul Magics. **WH flags several of these elite; WT/JW route the non-Stromgarde parts
  solo.** Skip Crystal in the Mountains (drop rate about 0.04%).
- **Desolace, L33-38, 6 srcs.** Ghost Walker Post, Shadowprey, Alliance Relations (Gelkis/Magram: kill 50
  Magram centaurs). Claim Rackmore's Treasure (nagas, finish on the island 36-38). IV uses Desolace
  only at 32-34; WH says STV has far higher quest density.

### 38-45
- **Scarlet Monastery, L33-42.** See [dungeon bundles](#scarlet-monastery-horde).
- **Faldir's Cove, Arathi L39-43, 2 srcs (WT, JW).** Land Ho!, Deep Sea Salvage, Drowned Sorrows, Sunken
  Treasure (simple escort, turns in at Booty Bay). Needs water breathing and swim speed.
- **Stonard and Misty Reed Post, Swamp of Sorrows L40-42, 4 srcs.** Lack of Surplus, Threat From the Sea,
  Galen's Escape (escort), Fresh Meat. Stonard flight path. Fel'zerul here also starts the Sunken
  Temple entry chain.
- **Badlands, L39-44, 4 srcs.** Study of the Elements: Rock chain (Nifty Stopwatch), Martek to Indurium,
  Badlands Reagent Run, Broken Alliances. Kargath flight path. **IV omits Badlands.**
- **Bloodsail Buccaneers, Up to Snuff, Scaring Shaky, Zanzil's Secret, STV L40-44, 3 srcs.** Booty Bay
  hub; Cracking Maury's Foot needs care.
- **Brackenwall, Dustwallow L41-45, 3 srcs.** Army of the Black Dragon, Identifying the Brood, Overlord
  Mok'Morokk's Concern.

### 44-50
- **Camp Mojache, Feralas L44-50, 5 srcs.** War on the Woodpaw chain, The Ogres of Feralas, Gordunni
  Cobalt, Zapped Giants, and the **Muisek Vessel chain** (Testing the Vessel, then Hippogryph, Faerie
  Dragon, Treant and Mountain Giant Muisek) ending in Weapons of Spirit.
- **Gadgetzan quests, Tanaris L43-48, 6 srcs.** Wastewander Justice, WANTED: Caliph Scorpidsting,
  Southsea Shakedown, The Dunemaul Compound, Gahz'ridian, Gadgetzan Water Survey. WIC: ~21,800 raw XP
  at 47. Flight path Gadgetzan.
- **Revantusk Village, Hinterlands L45-49, 5 srcs.** Eight to nine quests from one village
  (Snapjaws Mon!, Stalking the Stalkers, Cannibalistic Cousins, Hunt the Savages), the Rin'ji is Trapped!
  escort. Job Opening: Guard Captain (elite, 1 src) gives a melee trinket.
- **Searing Gorge, L47-52, 5 srcs.** Kalaran Windblade chain (Divine Retribution to Set Them Ablaze!) plus
  Thorium Point quests. WH: important "due to the zone's small size and density of quests". Two steps
  want a partner. Level placement differs by three levels between guides.
- **Blasted Lands, L47-52, 5 srcs.** Bloodmage Lynnore / Drazial repeatable beast-part quests (60-minute
  +25 stat buffs). WIC: 10 quests, ~24,000 raw XP. No flight path.

### 50-60
- **Un'Goro Crater, L48-56, 6 srcs (WT, WH, IV, WIC, WCL, GR).** Marshal's Refuge hub, crystal quests,
  the Fire Plume Ridge quests, A-Me 01 and Ringo escorts. Includes The Mighty U'cha (Beastslayer,
  see class notes). WIC: ~70,000 raw XP; never pull more than two mobs.
- **Videre Elixir / Linken chain, L55-57, 2 srcs (WT, WH).** Ends in Linken's Sword of Mastery or Spirit of
  Aquementas.
- **Felwood, L52-57, 5 srcs.** Emerald Sanctuary and Bloodvenom Post chains, Timbermaw Ally then Speak to
  Nafien, Cleansing Felwood. Opens the Timbermaw tunnel to Winterspring (needs rep). WIC: ~79,000 raw
  XP; do not take the escort solo.
- **Winterspring, L54-60, 5 srcs.** Everlook, Donova Snowden, Winterfall chains. WIC: up to 98,800 raw XP and
  recommends the yeti cave grind to 60.
- **Azshara, L50-54, optional, sources split.** WT and WCL use Betrayed and Kim'jael Indeed!; **WH says avoid
  it**, IV omits it. Only Hydraxian Waterlords is broadly considered worthwhile.
- **Western Plaguelands, L56-58, 5 srcs.** Scarlet Diversions, The Scourge Cauldrons and Target quests,
  All Along the Watchtowers, Andorhal. Araj is a level 61 elite.
- **Eastern Plaguelands, L58-60, 5 srcs.** Tirion Fordring line, Champion of the Banshee Queen,
  Darrowshire. Light's Hope flight path. Some quests need later-phase content (WH).
- **Burning Steppes, L55-58, 3 srcs.** The Rise of the Machines chain, Kibler's Exotic Pets, feeds the
  Blackrock Spire quests. **IV omits it.**
- **Silithus, L55-58, 3 srcs.** Twilight Mystery and related; **availability disagrees by phase** (WH: most
  need Phase 5; WT comment: not yet implemented).
- **Alterac Valley, L51+, 1 src (WCL).** AV quests start north of Tarren Mill; The Battle for Alterac
  leads to Hero of the Frostwolf. No XP-per-win figure was verified.

## Dungeon quest bundles

All need a group. XP figures are Warcraft Tavern / Zockify / Icy Veins, which agree with each other.
"Sum" is arithmetic over the listed quests.

### Ragefire Chasm (L9-18, 4 srcs)
Testing an Enemy's Strength 1,050; Searching for the Lost Satchel 880 then Returning the Lost Satchel
1,450; Slaying the Beast 1,150; The Power to Destroy... 1,450; Hidden Enemies (final leg) 1,150. Sum
about **7,130**. IV: little quest loot, "mainly completed for experience". WT suggests bringing a Tauren
and an Undead to share the Thunder Bluff and Undercity quests.

### Wailing Caverns (L17-25, 4 srcs)
Deviate Hides 1,600; Smart Drinks 1,350; Trouble at the Docks 1,350; Serpentbloom 1,700 (Thunder Bluff);
Deviate Eradication 2,050; The Glowing Shard 2,650; Leaders of the Fang 2,200. Sum about **12,900**.
IV recommends 18-25; WT runs it at 20-21; WIC chains it from 20 to 23.

### Shadowfang Keep (L22-30, 4 srcs)
The Book of Ur 2,100; Deathstalkers in Shadowfang 2,000; Arugal Must Die 3,300. Sum **7,400**. Far from the
Barrens; only worth it if the route passes the Sepulcher. WIC offers it as the alternative to Hillsbrad
at 25-27.

### Blackfathom Deeps (L24-30, 5 srcs)
Trouble in the Deeps 440 (prereq); The Essence of Aku'Mai 1,750; Allegiance to the Old Gods 2,650; Amongst
the Ruins 2,750; Baron Aquanis 3,050; Blackfathom Villainy 3,300. Sum **13,500**, excluding the
prerequisite. WT: finishing them all puts you "well into your 28th level". IV: an "awkward spot for
Horde"; stepping out to quest is viable.

### Razorfen Kraul (L30-40, 4 srcs)
A Vengeful Fate 4,050; An Unholy Alliance 2,800; Going, Going, Guano! 3,300; Blueleaf Tubers 2,100; Willix
the Importer 3,050. Sum **15,300**. Going, Going, Guano! is also the prerequisite for the Scarlet
Monastery quest Hearts of Zeal. If no group, WT says abandon and grind Thousand Needles harpies.

### Scarlet Monastery (Horde)
- **Into the Scarlet Monastery**, L33, 5 srcs. Start: Varimathras, Undercity. **5,150 XP.** Needs Loksey
  (Library), Herod (Armory), Mograine and Whitemane (Cathedral). Reward choice: Dragon's Blood Necklace,
  Prophetic Cane or **Sword of Omen**; this settles the reward the class notes had at "1 src".
- Side quests (WT XP): Hearts of Zeal 3,300, Vorrel's Revenge 3,300, Compendium of the Fallen 3,550, Test
  of Lore 2,100. IV: Graveyard rewards are "very poor".
- Timing: WT/WCL: Graveyard to 32, Library to 35-36, Armory to 39-40, Cathedral to 44-45. Nightfall's route
  inserts one SM run at 38.

### Razorfen Downs (L37-42, 2 srcs)
Bring the End 4,300; Extinguishing the Idol 4,250; An Unholy Alliance 3,500. WT: the better mid-30s dungeon
for Horde. IV and WIC route no RFD quests.

### Uldaman (L37-47, 3 srcs, disputed)
Necklace Recovery chain 19,550 (WT); The Platinum Discs 24,910; Reclaimed Treasures 3,600. **WIC recommends
it at 44-47; IV and WT say the Horde loot is poor and WT prefers RFD.**

### Zul'Farrak (L44-50, 6 srcs)
Gahz'rilla 7,100 (WT "Very Important"; IV: do it "for the speed bonus"), Divino-matic Rod 6,300, Tiara of
the Deep 6,050, The Spider God 5,850 (Horde only chain via Master Gadrin), Prophecy of Mosh'aru 5,250,
Scarab Shells 3,900, Troll Temper 3,900. IV: "one of the best dungeons to do a quest run of".
The **Prophecy of Mosh'aru / Hakkar chain** runs through ZF, Sunken Temple and Lower Blackrock Spire;
WT totals **79,850 XP** across the chain (4 srcs).

### Maraudon (L39-52, 5 srcs)
The Pariah's Instructions 5,450 (Mark of the Chosen; WT "Very Important"), Corruption of Earth and Seed 7,350,
Vyletongue Corruption 6,300, Twisted Evils 6,300, Shadowshard Fragments 3,450, Legends of Maraudon 3,400
then The Scepter of Celebras 5,700. WT: "definitely get a group" at 51.

### Sunken Temple (L46-55, 4 srcs)
Horde entry: Pool of Tears, The Atal'ai Exile, Return to Fel'Zerul, The Temple of Atal'Hakkar (7,100).
Neutral: The God Hakkar 7,900, Jammal'an the Prophet 7,900, the Marvon Rivetseeker chain (Stone Circle,
Into the Depths, Secret of the Circle). No attunement key exists. IV: finishing The Essence of Eranikus
costs the Chained Essence of Eranikus trinket; keep the trinket.

### Blackrock Depths and Spire (L52-60, 3 srcs each)
- **BRD:** Dark Iron Legacy 5,100 ("Very Important"), A Taste of Flame 6,200, Incendius! 5,800, Disharmony
  of Fire 7,300, The Princess Saved? 9,950, Operation: Death to Angerforge 7,750, Attunement to the Core
  6,600. IV's quest page returned 404, so none of this is IV-confirmed.
- **LBRS/UBRS:** Warlord's Command 8,300, Seal of Ascension 8,300, Kibler's Exotic Pets 6,400, Urok Doomhowl
  9,950, UBRS: For The Horde! 9,950. Warlord's Command starts the Onyxia attunement (WCL).
- **Key to Scholomance chain (4 srcs):** Scarlet Diversions, All Along the Watchtowers, Scholomance, Skeletal
  Fragments, Mold Rhymes With..., Fire Plume Forged, Araj's Scarab, The Key to Scholomance (9,950). A
  lockpicking Rogue can skip the key but "will miss out on all of the XP".
- **Scholomance:** Krastinov quests 6,600 each, Kirtonos 8,300, The Lich 9,950, Barov Family Fortune 6,600.
- **Stratholme:** Above and Beyond 8,300, Aurius' Reckoning 8,850, The Active Agent 6,850, Ramstein 6,600.

### Warrior

**Whirlwind Weapon chain, TOP for a duo or a level 38+ solo.** The chain does **not** start at Bath'rah, as
the older class notes say. It starts at the class trainer at 30:

1. **The Islander** (class trainer) sends you to Fray Island in the Barrens.
2. **The Affray**: Klannoc Macleod, Fray Island (about 68,48). Waves of challengers, then Big Will (L33).
   Reward: **Berserker Stance and Intercept**. Bring bandages and potions.
3. **The Windwatcher**: from Klannoc, sends you to **Bath'rah the Windwatcher**, Alterac Mountains (about 80,65).
4. **Cyclonian**: gather materials (below).
5. **The Summoning**: fight **Cyclonian, a level 40 elite air elemental**, at the cauldron by Bath'rah's hut.
6. **Whirlwind Weapon**: choice of Whirlwind **Axe**, **Sword** or **Warhammer**. CDB: quest level 40, requires
   30, 3,900 XP. WT and mmonerds.com recommend the Axe.

Materials (IV, WT, mmonerds.com, houstonaxe.com agree): 8 Liferoot; 30 Bloodscalp Tusk (STV trolls);
8 Thundering + 8 Burning + 8 Cresting Charm from Arathi Highlands exiles (level 38-39 elites per
mmonerds.com); the charms combine into 1 Essence of the Exile at the cauldron. Charms can be bought.

**Timing is the open question.** IV: start at 30, "by far the most powerful weapon a Warrior can wield at
Level 30", useful into the 40s. WT, WIC, mmonerds.com, WH: unlocked at 30 but a solo job at **38-40**. **ZK:
skip it** and take the next upgrade. MGW: Cyclonian needs help. The Berserker Stance half is worth doing
either way. Route implication: tusks in STV, charms in Arathi, Fray Island in the Barrens, Alterac at the end.

**Path of Defense (WORTH IT if tanking).** Class trainer, then Far Watch Post, then 5 Singed Scales from the
Lightning Ridge lizards. Arms leveling can skip it; IV says accept it at 10 anyway.

**Brutal Armor (OPTIONAL).** Thun'grim Firegaze, Crossroads mountain: 15 Smoky Iron Ingot, 10 Powdered
Azurite, 10 Iron Bar, 1 Vial of Phlogiston (Roogug, Razorfen Kraul). Only if RFK is on the route.

**Diamond Flask (OPTIONAL, 50+).** A Troubled Spirit, Warrior Kinship, War on the Shadowsworn, Voodoo
