# Notable Alliance quests and chains (1-60)

See [README.md](README.md) for the legend, source abbreviations and confidence caveats. Research
only: nothing here is loaded by the addon or turned into steps yet. Per Plan 3 a human reads this,
decides, and writes the step.

This file lists the quests and chains that leveling guides repeat as "big ticket": high XP for the
time, hubs worth clearing, chains that pay off later, and class chains that change how fast a
character levels. Weapon and gear rewards per class live in `../classes/`; this file adds the
**quest-chain detail** for the ones that matter and the general (all-class) chains.

Race-locked entries are marked (Human, Dwarf/Gnome, Night Elf). Classic Era has no Draenei, so
Azuremyst is out. Alliance Shamans do not exist in Classic Era.

## Levels 1-30

### Starter zones
- **Northshire / Elwynn (Human), L1-11, 3 srcs (WT, SG, OG).** A Threat Within, Kobold Camp Cleanup, Eagan
  Peltskinner, Wolves Across the Border; Milly's Harvest; Lost Necklace then Pie for Billy then Young
  Lovers; mine quests. All sources say to finish the starter zone to about 10-11. Bridge quests
  (Westbrook Garrison, Westfall Stew) hand you to Westfall. Per-quest XP unsourced.
- **Dun Morogh (Dwarf/Gnome), 2 srcs (WT, SG).** Frostmane Hold, Those Blasted Troggs / The Public
  Servant, Protecting the Herd (weapon upgrade; SG marks it group).
- **Teldrassil (Night Elf), 2 srcs (SG, WIC).** Crown of the Earth, Balance of Nature, Woodland Protector.
  WIC calls it the weakest start (auction house delayed). Night Elves go straight to Darkshore and
  stay to about 17-18 (WTC).

### Loch Modan, Westfall, Darkshore (11-20)
- **Loch Modan hub, L11-13, 4 srcs (WT, SG, WP, WIC).** Thelsamar Blood Sausages, Rat Catching, Stormpike's
  Order, In Defense of the King's Lands, The Trogg Threat. Enables cooking training. Filthy Paws is
  flagged a "HIGH DEATH ALERT" at 12. WIC has Dwarf/Gnome here at 12-20; WT at 9-11 then again at 22-23.
- **Westfall / Sentinel Hill hub, L12-16, 5 srcs (WT, SG, WP, WWG, WTC).** The Forgotten Heirloom, Westfall
  Stew, The Killing Fields, The People's Militia (two parts; part 2 flagged dangerous), Patrolling
  Westfall, Red Leather Bandanas, Keeper of the Flame, The Coastal Menace, A Swift Message then
  Continue to Stormwind. Start: Report to Gryan Stoutmantle. Skip Goretusk Liver Pie if slow. Timing
  differs: WT 15-16, SG 12-14, WTC 12-15, WIC 12-20.
- **The Defias Brotherhood, L14-18+, 7 srcs.** Seven-part chain from Gryan Stoutmantle, **2,600 XP**, reward
  choice Tunic, Chausses or Staff of Westfall. Runs through Duskwood and Westfall and ends killing
  Edwin VanCleef in Deadmines. IV: finish it before entering the dungeon; unlikely done before 18
  without a carry.
- **Darkshore hub (Auberdine), L11-20, 6 srcs.** Washed Ashore and beached creatures; the Buzzbox series;
  For Love Eternal (Anaya, respawns every 8 minutes); Bashal'Aran (3-4 parts); Plagued Lands; The
  Absent Minded Prospector (15-17, continues into Wetlands); The Tower of Althalaxx (3 parts, Staff of
  the Purifier; avoid the dungeon interior). WANTED: Murkdeep! needs a group. Timing: WT sends Humans
  at 18-20; LW keeps everyone here 12-17. Deep Ocean, Vast Sea: LW says skip, WT routes it.

### Redridge, Duskwood, Wetlands (16-30)
- **Redridge hub (Lakeshire), L16-24, 5 srcs.** Encroaching Gnolls then Assessing the Threat, Blackrock
  Menace, A Baying of Gnolls, Underbelly Scales, The Lost Tools, Murloc Poachers, Howling in the Hills.
  IV/NTB: Redridge or Ashenvale at 21-25. Redridge Goulash is slow (condor meat).
- **Duskwood hub (Darkshire), L18-28, 6 srcs.** Raven Hill, Deliveries to Sven then Sven's Revenge, Wolves
  at Our Heels, Eight Legged Menaces, The Totem of Infliction. Sub-chains below. WT's grind guide gives
  16-30k XP/hr at 21-30 at the Tranquil Garden and Raven Hill cemeteries (1 src). Mor'Ladim and Bride of
  the Embalmer need 3+; SG and D85 say abandon if ungrouped.
  - **The Legend of Stalvan**, 22-28, 4 srcs (GR, SG, D85, WWG). GR: 13 parts, "one of the greatest WoW
    Classic questlines"; SG: 2-2.5 hours, pulls you to Moonbrook, Goldshire and Stormwind.
  - **The Night Watch**, 4 srcs. Multi-stage undead grinding; later stages tougher.
  - **Look to the Stars**, L20-21, 3 srcs. 1-1.5 hours; 2+ for Insane Ghouls.
  - **The Hermit**, 22-35, 2 srcs (TG, D85). Long lore chain ending with Stitches (elite).
  - **Worgen in the Woods** (wand reward; see class notes).
- **Wetlands, L21-28, order disputed (WT 21-22, SG 23-24 and 27-28, IV pushes Menethil to 29-32).** Ormer's
  Revenge (Sarltooth elite; Raptor's End or Excavation Rod, 3 srcs); War Banners to **Defeat
  Nek'rosh** (level 26 elite, group; Ancient War Sword / Barreling Reaper, 3 srcs); The Cursed Crew
  then Lifting the Curse (2+ at the wreck; level 22 in SG vs 29 in WP). The Dark Iron War is the
  prerequisite that pushes The Fury Runs Deep to level 25 (IV).
- **Ashenvale (Astranaar), L20-25, 4 srcs.** The Zoram Strand (about 1,450 XP, snippet), Raene's Cleansing,
  Bathran's Hair, Ashenvale defense (Culling the Threat is elite). Ruuzel is an elite. No source
  recommends questing in the Barrens as Alliance.

### The Missing Diplomat (28+)
Starts at 28 (Thomas, Cathedral of Light, per a search summary), 11-12 steps running Stormwind to Duskwood
to Wetlands to Dustwallow Marsh. 4 srcs (GR, TG, SG, WWG). Mostly beyond 30; it is the story hand-off to
Theramore. SG calls the final fight "hard".

## Levels 31-60

Level = where the sources place it; they drift by 2-4 levels. LW (legacy-wow) and SG (Sage) both give
full routes for 32-60; IV points at Sage for zone routing, so they are not independent there.

### 30-40
- **Nesingwary chains, STV L30-45, 6 srcs (NTB, LW, SG, AL, JW, GR).** Welcome to the Jungle (Barnil
  Stonepot), Raptor Mastery (Hemet Nesingwary), Tiger Mastery (Ajeck Rouack, 3 parts), Panther Mastery
  (Sir S. J. Erlgadin). LW: Panther 1-3 and Tiger 1-3 at 32-35; Panther 4 (elite Bhag'thera) and Raptor 3
  at 38-40; Raptor 4 and **Big Game Hunter (King Bangalash)** at 42-43. The camp is at about (35, 10).
- **The Green Hills of Stranglethorn, L30 required, 6 srcs.** Barnil Stonepot. Collect four sets of
  manuscript pages. **4,700 XP** (WW). Reward: Jungle Boots, 3 Superior Healing Potion, 2 Thick Armor Kit.
  LW: turn in immediately after accepting, since pages are collected while doing other objectives.
- **Booty Bay hub, STV L32-43, 6 srcs.** Goblin Sponsorship (5 parts), Bloodscalp Ears, Singing Blue Shards,
  Krazek's Investigate the Camp, Supply and Demand then Some Assembly Required, The Bloodsail
  Buccaneers (5 parts), Scaring Shaky, Venture Company Mining, Akiris by the Bundle. **Traps:** Curse of
  the Tides, Voodoo Dues / Zanzil's Secret (SG: group, can abandon), Bloodsail captain hunt and
  Cortello's Riddle (group 2-3+), The Captain's Chest (elite Gorlash), Stranglethorn Fever (group 2+).
- **Rebel Camp, STV L32-35 and 42-43, 4 srcs.** The Second Rebellion, Bad Medicine, Krazek's Cookery, Return to
  Corporal Kaleb (first helm), Colonel Kurzen (cave elite with a stun, group). Special Forces:
  skipped in one LW page and done in another; treat as optional.
- **Shimmering Flats / Brassbolts, L31-35, 3 srcs.** The Brassbolts Brothers, Hardened Shells, Salt Flat
  Venom, Rocket Car Parts, Load Lightening, A Bump in the Road, Martek the Exiled, Goblin Sponsorship
  pt.1 and 5. Gahz'rilla starts here and feeds Zul'Farrak. PvP servers make the Flats as dangerous as STV.
- **Desolace, Nijel's Point, L34-42, 6 srcs for the zone, 3 for the chains.** Reclaimers' Business, Vahlarriel's
  Search (escort, group 2+), Centaur Bounty, Kodo Roundup, Bone Collector, Karnitol Shipwreck then Claim
  Rackmore's Treasure!, Scepter of Light and Book of the Ancients, Ghost-O-Plasm Round Up. Access is
  painful: GR routes boat Booty Bay to Ratchet then through Stonetalon; JW and BF say the trip goes through
  Horde land. SG says skip the Reagents for Reclaimers follow-up; LW does parts 3 and 4.
- **Arathi Highlands, Refuge Pointe, L36-40, 4 srcs.** Hints of a New Plague? (5 parts across Arathi and
  Hillsbrad), Worth Its Weight in Gold then Wand Over Fist, Northfold Manor, The Princess Trapped then
  Stones of Binding, Land Ho!, Deep Sea Salvage (needs water breathing), Drowned Sorrows, Sunken
  Treasure (escort; skip the pt.4 reward per LW). One snippet calls the Myzrael chain an elite worth
  grouping.
- **Dustwallow Marsh, Theramore, L35-40, 4 srcs.** The Deserters (elite, Balos Jacken tower), Suspicious
  Hoofprints, Daelin's Men, Lieutenant Paval Reethe, The Black Shield, Stinky's Escape ("very safe"),
  Mudrock Soup and Bugs, The Missing Diplomat.
- **Alterac via Southshore, L33-38, 3 srcs.** Dark Council, Noble Deaths, Crushridge Bounty. Low value: 1-1.5
  levels (BF).

### 40-50
- **Badlands, L39-42, 4 srcs.** Feeds Uldaman: Mirages, A Dwarf and His Tools, A Sign of Hope, Study of the
  Elements: Rock, This Is Going To Be Hard (group 2+, summons a level 45 elemental; Nifty Stopwatch),
  Barbecued Buzzard Wings, Indurium. Skip Tremors of the Earth's continuation (LW). Fewer Alliance
  quests than Horde (GR).
- **Swamp of Sorrows and Nethergarde, L41-43, 4 srcs.** Encroaching Wildlife, The Lost Caravan, Dream Dust in
  the Swamp, Noboru the Cudgel, Galen's Escape. No elite or group. Nethergarde flight path.
- **Gadgetzan hub, Tanaris L43-50, 6 srcs.** Wastewander Justice, Water Pouch Bounty, Gadgetzan Water
  Survey, WANTED: Caliph Scorpidsting and Andre Firebeard (elites with stealthed guards), Stoley's
  Shipment, Southsea Shakedown, The Dunemaul Compound, The Thirsty Goblin, Rise of the Silithid. Many
  lead into Zul'Farrak. LW: take Zanzil's Mixture and A Fool's Stout in Booty Bay before the boat.
- **Feralas, Feathermoon, L44-47, 5 srcs.** The Mark of Quality then Improved Quality, The Missing Courier,
  Against the Hatecrest, Against Lord Shalzaru, The Woodpaw Gnolls, Freedom For All Creatures then Doling
  Justice (group 2+), Zapped Giants, The Stave of Equinex. Skip the OOX-22/FE escort.
- **The Hinterlands, Aerie Peak, L46-48, 4 srcs.** Witherbark Cages, The Altar of Zul, Rhapsody chain, Skulk
  Rock Clean-Up, Troll Necklace Bounty, Rescue OOX-09/HL (escort), the pet chain. A snippet calls it
  "mediocre for questing"; guides use it as a two-level stop and for the chains that pass through.
- **Searing Gorge, Thorium Point, L46-51, 4 srcs.** The Flawless Flame then Forging the Shaft then The
  Flame's Casing (group 2-3+) then The Torch of Retribution then Squire Maltrake then Set Them Ablaze!;
  Suntara Stones; The Horn of the Beast; WANTED: Overseer Maltorius (group 3+).
- **Blasted Lands, Dreadmaul Hold, L45-50, 3 srcs.** Five collection quests (Basilisk's Bite, Vulture's Vigor,
  Snickerfang Jowls, A Boar's Vitality, The Decisive Striker); SG: all five from one grind spot.
- **Un'Goro Crater, L47-56, 6 srcs (NTB, GR, LW, SG, AL, snippet).** The Apes of Un'Goro, The Fare / Scent / Bait
  for Lar'korwi then **The Mighty U'cha** (L50, Beastslayer); Crystal Pylons (J.D. Collie; 7 Power
  Crystals of each of 4 colours); Marshal's Refuge (Chasing A-Me 01 escort, Muigin and Larion, Alien
  Ecology, Expedition Salvation, Shizzle's Flyer, Roll the Bones, Lost! escort); Krakle; Pawn Captures
  Queen and Calm Before the Storm (group for the Gorishi Hive Queen). NTB: "extremely high quest
  density". GR: stay until about 53. AL: "Ignore the followup" twice.
- **Linken's chain, L48-57, 4 srcs.** It's a Secret to Everybody, The Videre Elixir, Meet at the Grave, A
  Grave Situation, Linken's Sword, A Gnome's Assistance, Linken's Memory, Linken's Adventure. Ends in
  a trinket (snippet: a chance to stun). Only worth it if the route already passes all four zones.

### 50-60
- **Felwood, L48-57, 5 srcs.** Timbermaw Ally then Speak to Nafien, Cleansing Felwood, Verifying the
  Corruption, Felbound Ancients then Ancient Spirit, Dousing the Flames of Protection then A Final
  Blow, Cleansed Water Returns to Felwood, Well of Corruption, To Winterspring!. The Timbermaw cave
  in the northeast is how guides reach Winterspring. Shadow Hold section is group 2+.
- **Burning Steppes, L48-58, 4 srcs.** Extinguish the Firegut, Dragonkin Menace (starts the Onyxia
  attunement; SG conflicts on solo vs group), Broodling Essence, Tablet of the Seven, The Smoldering
  Ruins of Thaurissan. **The True Masters** is a five-part chain between Lakeshire and Stormwind (LW).
- **Western Plaguelands, L50-58, 5 srcs.** Chillwind Camp (about 42,84). A Call to Arms: The Plaguelands!,
  The Scourge Cauldrons and the Target quests (Felstone Field is group 2+), All Along the Watchtowers,
  A Plague Upon Thee (from L48), Better Late Than Never, Andorhal chain. Unfinished Business, Alas,
  Andorhal and Araj's Scarab need 3+. SG visits it at 50-51 and 56-58.
- **Eastern Plaguelands, L55-60, 5 srcs.** Light's Hope hub. Demon Dogs, Blood Tinged Skies, Carrion Grubbage,
  the **Pamela's Doll chain** (Darrowshire), the **Tirion Fordring chain** (Redemption, Of Forgotten
  Memories, Of Lost Honor, Of Love and Family; middle steps group 2+). Hearth bind at Light's Hope
  after Argent Dawn rep.
- **Winterspring, L53-60, 6 srcs.** Are We There, Yeti?, Chillwind Horns then Return to Tinkee, The Everlook
  Report, the Threat of the Winterfall chain (Donova Snowden), Kel'Theril quests, High Chief Winterfall
  (elite, 10-minute respawn). TG says Alliance "get more traction here". LW ends its whole route here.
- **Silithus, L56-60, 4 srcs, phase-dependent.** LW: Taking Back Silithus, Securing the Supply Lines, Deadly
  Desert Venom, The Twilight Mystery, Umber, Archivist. SG describes a different Phase 1-4 versus Phase
  5 path. GR/NTB: alternate finish if EPL is contested.
- **Azshara, L45-54, low priority, 3 srcs.** Thin for Alliance; WT visits it only at 53-54.
- **Alterac Valley, L51+.** Snippet only; no XP rate sourced.

## Dungeon quest bundles

All need a group. XP is Zockify / Warcraft Tavern / IV unless noted. "Sum" is arithmetic over the values shown.

### Deadmines (L14-21, 4 srcs)
Collecting Memories 1,350; Oh Brother... 1,550; Underground Assault 1,550; Red Silk Bandanas 1,250; The Defias
Brotherhood 2,600; The Unsent Letter 870 (starts the Stockade Riots chain). Sum about **9,170**. Level band
disputed: NTB 15-21, WTC 18+, IV finish at 18+. WT recommends no dungeons before the Stockade at 24-29; SG
does not run Deadmines at all.

### Stormwind Stockade (L22-30, 5 srcs; Alliance only)
| Quest | Level | XP |
|---|---|---|
| The Stockade Riots | 16 | 2,350 |
| Quell The Uprising | 22 | 2,650 |
| The Color of Blood | 22 | 2,650 |
| What Comes Around... | 22 | 2,000 |
| The Fury Runs Deep | 22 or 25 | 2,750 |
| Crime and Punishment | 22 | 2,100 |

Sum about **14,500**. IV: one of the shortest instances, clearable in under 30 minutes. Horde has no quests
there. The Fury Runs Deep gives Headbasher. The Riots chain leads to the Seal of Wrynn (2 srcs for the
tail). Level band disputed: WT 24-29, SG 25-27, IV 25-30, WIC 28-30, WTC 17+.

### Blackfathom Deeps (L18-25, 4 srcs)
Knowledge in the Deeps 2,750 (level 10 in IV/ZK, 18 in WT-D), Researching the Corruption 2,400, Blackfathom
Villainy 3,300, Twilight Falls 2,550, In Search of Thaelrid 2,400. About **11,000** for the first four.
Minimum level 20 to complete all (IV). NTB puts it at 24-32; WT and SG do not route it.

### Gnomeregan (L20-25, 3 srcs)
Gyrodrillmatic Excavationators 2,450, Gnogaine 2,200, The Only Cure is More Green Glow 2,450, Save Techbot's
Brain! 2,650, Essential Artificials 3,050, The Grand Betrayal 2,750, Data Rescue 3,650. Sum about
**19,200**. No leveling route surveyed puts Gnomeregan in its path. Use all four Punchograph machines in one
run for Data Rescue.

### Razorfen Kraul (Alliance side, 3 srcs)
Mortality Wanes (L25, 3,050 XP), The Crone of the Kraul (L29, 3,350 XP; needs Lonebrow's Journal first).

### Shadowfang Keep
No Alliance quests apart from class quests (3 srcs). Skip for quest XP.

### Scarlet Monastery
- **In the Name of the Light**, quest level 34, **4,700 XP**, 3 srcs (IV, WT, SG). Raleigh the Devout,
  Southshore. Kill bosses from the Library, Armory and Cathedral. Reward choice: Sword of Serenity, Bonebiter,
  Black Menace or Orb of Lorica. Prerequisite chain **Down the Scarlet Path**; where it starts is disputed
  (IV: Brother Anton in Stormwind; a snippet: Brother Crowley in Stormwind, then Anton at Nijel's Point; LW
  and SG place it at the Nijel's Point inn).
- **Mythology of the Titans**, level 28, 3,550 XP plus 150 Ironforge rep, 3 srcs. Librarian Mae Paledust,
  Hall of Explorers, Ironforge; book is in the Library. A cheap add-on to any SM run.
- Disagreement: NTB and WT call SM the best 35-45 method; SG says optional and not recommended for Alliance;
  BF says access is difficult.

### Razorfen Downs
Bring the Light, level 39, 4,300 XP, Archbishop Benedictus, Stormwind Cathedral (2 srcs, WT only).

### Uldaman (L35-47, 5 srcs)
Agmond's Fate (L30 chain, Prospector Stormpike), A Sign of Hope (L35 chain, needs Badlands quests first), The
Shattered Necklace (L37), The Lost Dwarves (L35), The Platinum Discs (L40), Reclaimed Treasures (L33),
Uldaman Reagent Run (L38). LW: 75-100% of a level per run. IV quest levels 30-40; WT and NTB route it 42-47.

### Zul'Farrak (L44-54, 5 srcs)
Divino-matic Rod (L40), Gahz'rilla (L40; Carrot on a Stick), Scarab Shells, Troll Temper, Tiara of the Deep,
and the Alliance-only **Nekrum's Medallion chain** (below). WT ranks ZF above Maraudon and Sunken Temple.
- **Nekrum's Medallion** (3 srcs): Witherbark Cages (Gryphon Master Talonaxe, Hinterlands), The Altar of Zul,
  Thadius Grimshade, Nekrum's Medallion (**5,250 XP**, 150 Wildhammer/Ironforge rep; Gryphon Rider's
  Stormhammer or Leggings), The Divination, Return to the Hinterlands, Saving Sharpbeak.
- **Prophecy of Mosh'aru chain** (3 srcs): Screecher Spirits, The Prophecy of Mosh'aru, The Ancient Egg, The God
  Hakkar (Sunken Temple), The Lost Tablets of Mosh'aru, The Final Tablets (LBRS). One snippet gives 79,850 XP
  for the whole chain; the Horde draft also found that figure, in WT.

### Maraudon (L39-52, 3 srcs)
The Pariah's Instructions (L39; Mark of the Chosen), Shadowshard Fragments (L39), Legends of Maraudon (L41),
Vyletongue Corruption (L41), **Corruption of Earth and Seed** (L45; Thrash Blade "one of the best one-hand
weapons, even in the end game", Resurgence Rod, Verdant Keeper's Aim). WT ranks ZF above it.

### Sunken Temple attunement (Alliance, L38-47, 3 srcs)
In Search of The Temple (Stormwind), To The Hinterlands, Gryphon Master Talonaxe, Rhapsody Shindigger,
Rhapsody's Kalimdor Kocktail, Rhapsody's Tale, Into The Temple of Atal'Hakkar (10 Atal'ai Tablets). LW:

the Prophet (L38), The God Hakkar (L40). **There is no Marshal Maxwell step in this chain**; he is in the
Burning Steppes.

### Stratholme, Scholomance, Blackrock Spire (L52-60, 2 srcs, IV)
- **Stratholme Live:** Houses of the Holy, Of Love and Family, The Archivist, The Great Ezra Grimm, The Restless

## Class-specific chains

Priority tags: **TOP** = changes how fast the class levels, **WORTH IT**, **OPTIONAL**. Starters are
Alliance-side. Reward and gear detail is in [`../classes/`](../classes/README.md); this section adds chain
order, starters, costs and group needs. Alliance Hunters are Dwarf or Night Elf; Alliance Druids are Night
Elf only. Shaman does not exist for Alliance in Classic Era.

**Cross-class finding:** the free mounts (Paladin, Warlock at 40) are the biggest quest-driven speed wins;
