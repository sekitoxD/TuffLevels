# Shaman — notable leveling items (1-60)

See [README.md](README.md) for the legend, abbreviations and confidence caveats.

Classic Era Shamans are **Horde only** (Orc/Tauren/Troll). Enhancement (2H then dual-wield after
Windfury) is the standard leveling route, so this is a **2H weapon progression**, with a caster
staff alternative for Elemental/Restoration. Most of the good 2H weapons are shared with Warriors
and Paladins (see [notable_warrior_items.md](notable_warrior_items.md)).

## Top priority

### Scarlet Monastery weapons — level 37-39, D (Herod / Mograine / Armory)
- **Ravager** (37.3 DPS 2H axe, 25% Herod), **Mograine's Might** (38.9 DPS 2H mace,
  Scarlet Commander Mograine), and **Illusionary Rod** (Library) — all "very strong" for Shamans
  (IV/WH). Drops, not guaranteed; SM is where the bracket's upgrades cluster.

### Zum'rah's Vexing Cane — caster staff, Zul'Farrak
- IV recommends it for **caster Shamans**: "all the stats you need while leveling". Comes from
  Zul'Farrak; the source gives no boss or drop rate (the name suggests Witch Doctor Zum'rah — verify).

### Totem quests (utility, not gear)
- **Earth Totem** (L4), **Fire Totem** (L10), **Water Totem** (L20 — a long chain; Healing Stream /
  Mana Tide), **Air Totem** (L30 — simple; Windfury / Grace of Air) (WT). The L30 Air Totem quest
  is the one that unlocks Windfury Totem.
