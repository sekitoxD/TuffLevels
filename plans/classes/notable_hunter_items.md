# Hunter — notable leveling items (1-60)

See [README.md](README.md) for the legend, abbreviations and confidence caveats.

Hunters level on their **ranged weapon**, so this list is the ranged-weapon progression plus ammo.
The source guides (WIC, WT) cover **bows only**; guns were not documented — see Open items.

## Top priority: worth a detour or a deliberate pick-up

### Master Hunter's Bow — level 25, Q, both factions
- **Quest:** "Big Game Hunter" — Stranglethorn Vale. 19.4 DPS, a large jump over the ~12 DPS
  available before it (WIC, WT, WH/search).
- Wowhead also has a **Master Hunter's Rifle** (item 17687) alongside the bow (item 17686); this
  is very likely the gun option from the same quest but that was **not confirmed** — verify on the
  quest page. Relevant for Dwarf/Gnome/Orc/Troll Hunters who prefer guns.

### Verdant Keeper's Aim — level 45, Q-dungeon, both factions
- **Quest:** "Corruption of Earth and Seed" — Maraudon. 28.2 DPS. Needs a Maraudon run (group).
- Only worth it if Maraudon is on the route anyway (WIC, WT).

## Non-weapon and utility

- **Call of the Raptor** (L35, Gryfe, Stranglethorn Vale, both factions): choice of Headhunter's
  Bands (wrist) or Voodoo Hunting Bow. *Single source (NTB) — verify; the bow name was not
  confirmed elsewhere.*
- **Devilsaur Eye** trinket (L50+, both): "The Hunter's Charm" chain from a class trainer → Azshara →
  Temple of Atal'Hakkar. IV: "the best on-use trinket that you will have access to for a long time".
  Requires Sunken Temple.
- **Tame Beast** (L10 quest, race-specific trainer) — ability unlock, effectively mandatory.
- **Rhok'delar** (Ancient Sinew Tied chain, L50+, Azgora (H, Barrens) / Huntsman Markhor (A, Burning
  Steppes)) — the legendary bow is a level-60 endgame item; outside "fastest to 60".
- Stat guidance: Agility > Intellect > Spirit for pure leveling (1 Agi = 2 ranged AP) per WIC.

## Open items

- **Guns are not researched** — the WIC/WT tables only list bows. The Master Hunter's Rifle,
  Sniper Rifle (green, L39, from Razorfen Downs undead / Uldaman chests) and Sharpshooter Harquebus
  (L55, containers in BRS/DM/BRD) surfaced but are weak evidence. Gun coverage is the main gap for
  this class and needs a proper pass on a gun-specific guide.
- Melee weapon for hunters (Raptor Strike / stat stick) not covered.
- Level for several low-level quest bows was not stated by the source.
