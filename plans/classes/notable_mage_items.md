# Mage — notable leveling items (1-60)

See [README.md](README.md) for the legend, abbreviations and confidence caveats.

Mages level by casting; the **wand** is the filler damage and Intellect/Spirit gear the stat base
(WIC: Spirit > Intelligence > Stamina for leveling). Mage class quests are the best-documented
"go get this item" quests of any caster class, all by way of **Tabetha in Dustwallow Marsh**.

## Top priority: worth a dedicated detour

### Celestial Orb / Celestial Stave — level 35+, Q-chain (needs an Uldaman run), both factions
- **Chain** (NTB): Return to the Marsh → The Infernal Orb → The Exorcism → Power in Uldaman → Mana
  Surges → Celestial Power. Starts from the mage class trainer, then Tabetha in **Dustwallow Marsh**;
  the Uldaman step requires a full group. The last steps are "Mana Surges" and "Celestial Power"
  (WH/search confirms Mana Surges is the last stage, min. level 35).
- **Reward, choose one:**
  - **Celestial Orb** — off-hand with bonus Intellect and a **mana-restore proc on a 30-minute
    cooldown**; usable at 60. Guides recommend the Orb because casters have few off-hands (WH, IV).
  - **Celestial Stave** — 2H staff with solid spell damage.
- **Route implication:** Dustwallow + Uldaman; only worth building in if Uldaman is on the route.

### Mage's Wand chain (Icefury / Ragefire / Nether Force Wand) — level 30+, Q-chain, both factions
- **Chain** (NTB): Journey to the Marsh → Hidden Secrets → Get the Scoop → Rituals of Power → Items
  of Power → Mage's Wand.
- **Reward, choose one** (all 44-83 damage, ~26.6 DPS): Icefury Wand (frost), Ragefire Wand (fire),
  Nether Force Wand (arcane).
- **Requirements:** visit Tabetha in Dustwallow Marsh; buy Jade; farm 10 Witherbark Totem Sticks and
  perform a ritual in the Arathi Highlands; retrieve the *Rituals of Power* book from the
  **Scarlet Monastery Library**. The Library step means a dungeon wing, likely a group.
- WT lists the Icefury Wand as a level-30 Mage class-quest reward; IV lists it under "Journey to the
  Marsh" too — the three sources agree.

### Sunken Temple chain — level 50+, Q-chain
- Magecraft → Magic Dust → The Siren's Coral → Destroy Morphaz. Rewards a **choice of Fire Ruby
  (trinket) or Arcane Crystal Pendant (neck)**. Requires travelling to Azshara, killing blood elves
  for magic dust, farming naga for coral, and a Sunken Temple run to kill Morphaz. Late, expensive.

### Arcane Refreshment — level 60, Dire Maul North
- Rewards the strongest Conjure Water rank. Out of scope for "to 60".

- **Starts (resolves the "Confirm the Celestial chain start" open item):** both Marsh chains are started by the
  **mage class trainers** (Journey to the Marsh: Deino, Jennea Cannon; Return to the Marsh: Anastasia Hartwell, Bink,
  Uthel'nay, Jennea Cannon). Tabetha is where the middle and last steps turn in. NTB is right, the "begins with
  Tabetha" sources are not. Magecraft is started by the trainers too; Destroy Morphaz is started by Archmage
  Xylem (map 1, Azshara).
- **CONFLICT: "Items of Power" is not in the wand chain.** The note puts it between Rituals of Power and Mage's
  Wand. In the DB, Items of Power (1948, min 30) has no prerequisite and no next-in-chain; it is started and
  turned in by Tabetha, and Mage's Wand (1952) requires **Rituals of Power (1951)**. Whether Items of Power is
  a required side quest is not answered by the DB; check in game. Hidden Secrets turns in to Magus Tirth.
- **CONFLICT: wand damage.** The three wands are 37.1 (Ragefire), 37.2 (Icefury) and 37.3 dps (Nether Force) in
  the DB, not "~26.6".
- **CONFLICT: the Sunken Temple reward is a choice of three, not two:** Glacial Spike (20035, 1H dagger, 34.6
  dps) as well as Fire Ruby (20036, trinket) and Arcane Crystal Pendant (20037, neck).
- Arcane Refreshment (7463) exists, min 60, both factions. The Arcanist's Cookbook (7500) is a min 54 Mage quest.
