# Paladin — notable leveling items (1-60)

See [README.md](README.md) for the legend, abbreviations and confidence caveats.

Classic Era Paladins are **Alliance only** (Human/Dwarf); every quest here is an Alliance quest.
Paladins level on a slow **two-hander** (Retribution/Reckoning), so weapon DPS drives speed.

## Top priority: worth a dedicated detour

### Verigan's Fist — level 20, Q-chain, Alliance Human/Dwarf Paladin
- **What:** 2H mace. Guides say it can stay until level 35-40 and treat it as *the* rite-of-passage
  weapon for leveling Paladins (WH/search, IV, WIC).
- **Chain** (Ten Ton Hammer / Wowhead via search):
  1. **The Tome of Valor** — from Duthorian Rall (Stormwind Cathedral) or Brandur Ironhammer
     (Ironforge, Mystic Ward). Gives you the Tome item.
  2. Read the Tome → second **The Tome of Valor** quest: go to **Daphne Stilwell at Longshore in
     Westfall** and defend her from waves of Defias. Solo-able at 20 with care; easier with a friend.
     Rewards the spell *Sense Undead* and the shield *Bastion of Stormwind*.
  3. **The Test of Righteousness** — the weapon is forged by the blacksmith **Jordan Stilwell
     outside Ironforge** from four rare materials: Whitestone Oak Lumber, Jordan's Smithing
     Hammer, Jordan's Refined Ore Shipment and a Purified Kor Gem.
- **Note:** IV lists the chain under the name "Sense Undead" (the spell reward of step 2), which is
  a common source of confusion when searching.
- **Route implication:** three zones (Stormwind/Westfall/Dun Morogh) plus material gathering;
  a level-20 detour that pays off across roughly 15-20 levels. Author as a `class = "PALADIN"`
  bundle. **Material drop locations were not captured** — look them up before authoring.

### Doomulus Prime — level 58, Q, all classes that can use 2H
- "The Perfect Poison" (Silithus). 55.7 DPS 2H, the best quest 2H in the bracket.

## Non-weapon notes

- **Forging the Mightstone** (Sunken Temple, L50+; Commander Ashlam Valorfist, Western Plaguelands):
  choice of Lightforged Blade, Sanctified Orb, or Chivalrous Signet (IV). Dungeon quest — not a
  speed-leveling detour.
- **Utility quests that speed leveling** (ability unlocks, not gear): *Redemption* (L12); the
  *Summon Warhorse* mount quest at L40 — IV calls it long and expensive but cheaper than the vendor
  mount. The epic Charger chain (Dire Maul and Scholomance steps, L50+; IV) is out of scope.
- Stat guidance from WIC: Strength > Stamina > Intellect > Agility > Spirit for the Reckoning build.
