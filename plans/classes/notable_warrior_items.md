# Warrior — notable leveling items (1-60)

See [README.md](README.md) for the legend, abbreviations and confidence caveats.

Warriors level mostly on a **two-hander** (Arms); the guides are unanimous that the weapon matters
more than any armor slot, and that most of the best leveling weapons are **axes**. So this list is
weapon-first.

## Top priority: worth a dedicated detour

### Whirlwind Axe — level 30, Q-chain, both factions
- **What:** 2H axe, ~35.6 DPS. IV calls it "by far the most powerful weapon a Warrior can wield at
  Level 30"; WIC/IV say it lasts until the 40s and should be started the moment you hit 30.
- **Chain:** The Windwatcher → Cyclonian → The Summoning → Whirlwind Weapon. Starts with
  **Bath'rah the Windwatcher in the Alterac Mountains** (IV).
- **Prerequisite:** the Berserker Stance class quest must be done first (IV, 1 src — check the
  Wowhead quest page for the exact requirement before writing the step).
- **Gathering cost** (IV):
  - 8 Liferoot (farm or buy)
  - 30 Bloodscalp Tusks (Bloodscalp trolls, Stranglethorn Vale)
  - 1 Essence of the Exile — made in a cauldron from 8 each of Thundering, Burning and Cresting
    Charms, dropped by the exiles in the Arathi Highlands
- **Route implication:** the materials span Stranglethorn Vale and Arathi Highlands, so the chain
  is best slotted where the route already passes through both. Good candidate for a
  `class = "WARRIOR"` bundle of `accept` / `complete` / `turnin` steps with a `note` listing the
  materials.
- Sources: IV (quest chain + materials); WIC and ZK both list the axe at level 30.

### Diamond Flask — level 50+, Q-chain via Sunken Temple, both factions
- Trinket, "one of the very best trinkets for Warrior" (IV). Chain: A Troubled Spirit → Warrior
  Kinship → War on the Shadowsworn → Voodoo Feathers (Blasted Lands / Sunken Temple).
- Requires Sunken Temple, so treat as an optional late-game step, not a speed detour.

## Bracket cheat-sheet (what the guides say to aim for)

- **1-10:** vendor Large Axe (L3) → Elunite Axe (L10) via the race's starter chain.
- **10-20:** Alliance: Edge of the People's Militia → Miner's Revenge / Staff of Westfall (Deadmines).
  Horde: Axe of Orgrimmar / Wind Rider Staff → Crescent Staff (Wailing Caverns) → Demolition Hammer.
- **20-30:** Ancient War Sword / Archeus / Corpsemaker (RFK), then **Whirlwind Axe at 30**.
- **30-40:** Whirlwind Axe carries you; Scarlet Monastery (Bonebiter A / Ravager both) is the next jump.
- **40-50:** Grim'lok's Charge, Chief's Enforcer, Resurgence Rod (Maraudon).
- **50-60:** Beastslayer / Limb Cleaver → Dreadforge Retaliator (BRD) → Doomulus Prime at 58.

## Notes for route authoring

- Whirlwind Axe is the only Warrior item here that truly justifies a detour by itself; everything
  else is either on-route quest rewards or a dungeon drop.
- Every quest reward above is a plain `accept`/`turnin` on a quest the route probably already
  takes. Those need no `class` filter unless the reward choice matters only to Warriors, in which
  case a `note` on the turn-in step is enough.
- Dual-wielding is called out by the guides as not worth it while leveling (hit penalty) unless the
  1H weapons far exceed the best 2H.

**Whirlwind chain (confirmed).** Order is The Islander (1718) → The Affray (1719) → The Windwatcher (1791) →
Cyclonian (1712) → The Summoning (1713) → Whirlwind Weapon (1792). All six are Warrior-only, `MinLevel` 30, no
race limit.
- **CONFLICT with "Chain" above:** the chain does **not** start with Bath'rah. The Islander is started by the
  class trainers (six of them, both factions) and turned in to **Klannoc Macleod** (Fray Island, Barrens, map 1).
  Klannoc starts The Affray (Kill Big Will) and The Windwatcher; Bath'rah (Alterac Mountains, map 0) only turns
  in The Windwatcher and starts Cyclonian onward. The reward is a choice of Whirlwind Axe (6975), Whirlwind
  Warhammer (6976) or Whirlwind Sword (6977), all two-handers.
- Cyclonian materials match: 8 Liferoot (3357), 30 Bloodscalp Tusk (3901), 1 Essence of the Exile (6851).
  The Summoning needs a Whirlwind Heart (6894).
- Whirlwind Axe (6975): 102-154, 3.60 speed, 35.6 dps, +15 Strength +14 Stamina, Warrior-only, BoP.
  **`RequiredLevel` is 0** in the DB (item level 40); the level 30 gate comes from the quests' `MinLevel`.
- Not answered by the DB: whether "the Berserker Stance quest" is The Affray or a separate step, and how long
  the elite fights take. Confirm in game.
