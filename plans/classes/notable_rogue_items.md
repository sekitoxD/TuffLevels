# Rogue — notable leveling items (1-60)

See [README.md](README.md) for the legend, abbreviations and confidence caveats.

Also see **Model-derived additions** at the bottom: weapons a DPS model ranks highly that the tables
below do not list, with sources checked against the DB.

The weapon is the single most important piece of gear for a leveling Rogue (WH). Two common builds:
**Combat Swords** (the standard leveling spec — sword list) and **Subtlety/dagger** (more crowd
control, popular on hardcore/PvP realms — dagger list). Rogue class quests give **skills, not gear**
(poisons, lockpicking) — the gear comes from general quest rewards and dungeons (NTB, IV).

## Top priority: worth a detour or a deliberate pick-up

### Sword of Serenity (A) / Sword of Omen (H) — level 33-34, Q-dungeon
- **Alliance:** "In the Name of the Light" — Scarlet Monastery, given by Raleigh the Devout in
  Southshore, requires level 34. Choice of **Sword of Serenity** (30.0 DPS 1H sword), **Bonebiter**,
  **Black Menace** (29.7 DPS dagger) or Orb of Lorica. Requires killing Whitemane, Mograine, Herod
  and Loksey — a full SM run, so group (3 srcs; the reward list is confirmed).
- **Horde:** "Into the Scarlet Monastery" → Sword of Omen (29.7 DPS). *1 src (WIC) — verify quest
  name and reward list.*
- A roughly 30 DPS weapon is a ~+7 DPS jump over the mid-20s options; this is the single biggest
  swing for a Combat Rogue between 30 and 45.

### Vanquisher's Sword — level 37, Q, both factions (dungeon quest)
- Razorfen Downs dungeon quest, 30.0 DPS 1H sword. Quest name **disagrees between sources**: WIC
  gives "Bring the End" (A table) and "Bring the Light" (H table); WT gives "Bring the Light" for
  Alliance. Verify on the item's Wowhead page.

### Corruption of Earth and Seed — level 45, Q-dungeon (Maraudon)
- Thrash Blade (35.2 DPS 1H sword) is on the Rogue list. Needs a Maraudon run.

### Azsharite Weaponry — level 55, Q, Stranglethorn Vale
- Enchanted Azsharite Felbane **Sword** (35.8 DPS) or **Dagger** (35.7 DPS). Both factions.

### Blade of Reckoning — level 53, Q (Blackrock Depths)
- "Jail Break!" — 35.8 DPS 1H sword. Both factions.

## Non-weapon and class-quest notes

- **Poisons quest** (L20): A "Mathias and the Defias" (Mathias Shaw, Stormwind) / H "Mission:
  Possible But Not Probable" (Shenthul, Orgrimmar) — unlocks poisons (needs 75 Lockpicking);
  **no gear** (NTB, IV). The Thistle Tea questline is the other class quest in IV.
- **Ravenholdt questline** (L50+): IV lists Whisperwalk Boots and Duskbat Drape as rewards that
  "have their own place in end gear sets", and Ebon Mask (replaced early in endgame). Best
  non-weapon rogue quest reward; timing/location of the chain start was not captured.
- **Lockpicking** (L16+): skill, not gear.
- Dual-wield leveling means the off-hand matters too; the tables above are the main-hand lists and
  the guides don't separately list off-hand upgrades.

## Model-derived additions (2026-09-20)

Produced by an offline DPS calculator (`tools/tuffweights`, Classic Era 1.12 rules, all rogue-usable weapons
in either hand, five builds: combat swords/maces/fist, dagger assassination/subtlety) and then checked by hand
against the DB with `tools/qdb.py`. The calculator's output is not committed (it is derived from the DB); this
section keeps only what a human verified. Nothing above was edited.

**How far to trust it.** It scores damage only (no stamina, armor, utility, convenience), on a 20-second
fight with maxed weapon skill, and it does not know factions. Weapons within ~1% of each other are ties.
Level ranges are the levels where the item is in the model's top 5 (of ~970 weapons) for a build; the first
level is the item's or quest's minimum level.

### Do not plan around (in the model's shortlist, but not obtainable in practice)

- **Scout's Blade / Sentinel's Blade** (many level versions, 19542-19549, 20441, 20443): sold by Kelm Hargunth and
  Illiyana Moonblaze, and the vendor rows require **Revered** with Warsong Outriders or Silverwing Sentinels (a Warsong
  Gulch reputation grind). The calculator first treated them as plain vendor items; the export now records the
  vendor condition and no longer scores them as freely buyable.
- **Crystal Tipped Stiletto** (20657): drops from Crimson Templar, a level-60 elite with no spawn rows, i.e.
  a Scourge Invasion event mob. Not leveling content.
- **Doomforged Straightedge** (12535): Blackrock Depths trash, ~0.02%. **Ironfoe** (11684): Emperor Dagran Thaurissan, 1%.
  Both would be top options at 49+/55+ if they dropped, which is a lottery ticket, not a plan.
- **Krol Blade** (2244): drops from the level-63 Colossus of Ashi/Regal/Zora (Ahn'Qiraj-era) and from the world-drop
  table (~0.001%). It would add about +5% at 51-59, but nothing while levelling can reach it. `Rogue.upgrades` now files it
  under "worth it if convenient": equip one if it turns up, never plan around it.

### Effort classes (what `Rogue.upgrades` uses)

`tuffweights run --faction horde` also writes `worth.md`. For each weapon it measures how much DPS it adds over the best
loadout built only from vendor and quest weapons (what you wear anyway), best across builds, then sorts by how hard it is
to get: **detour** = vendor/quest source with mean uplift >= 2.5% and mean x levels >= 20 percent-levels;
**convenient** = adds >= 1% somewhere but is a drop or a small gain; **skip** = under 1% everywhere. The model cannot see
quest-chain length, group requirements or that many quest rewards sit inside dungeons, so the classes were confirmed by hand.
Horde detours: Wingblade (15-20, ~+17%), Outlaw Sabre (21-24, ~+14%), Sword of Omen (33-36, ~+7%), Vanquisher's Sword
(37-48, +3% mean), Thrash Blade (45-50, +3.5%). The vendor/quest baseline is thin below level 25, so early uplifts are
overstated.
