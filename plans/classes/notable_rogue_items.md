# Rogue — notable leveling items (1-60)

See [README.md](README.md) for the legend, abbreviations and confidence caveats.

The weapon is the single most important piece of gear for a leveling Rogue (WH). Two common builds:
**Combat Swords** (the standard leveling spec — sword list) and **Subtlety/dagger** (more crowd
control, popular on hardcore/PvP realms — dagger list). Rogue class quests give **skills, not gear**
(poisons, lockpicking) — the gear comes from general quest rewards and dungeons (NTB, IV).

## Top priority: worth a detour or a deliberate pick-up

### Sword of Serenity (A) / Sword of Omen (H) — level 33-34, Q-dungeon
- **Alliance:** "In the Name of the Light" — Scarlet Monastery, given by Raleigh the Devout in
  Southshore, requires level 34. Choice of **Sword of Serenity** (30.0 DPS 1H sword), **Bonebiter**,
  **Black Menace** (29.7 DPS dagger) or Orb of Lorica. Requires killing Whitemane, Mograine, Herod
  and Loksey — a full SM run, so group (3 srcs; the reward list is confirmed).
- **Horde:** "Into the Scarlet Monastery" → Sword of Omen (29.7 DPS). *1 src (WIC) — verify quest
  name and reward list.*
- A roughly 30 DPS weapon is a ~+7 DPS jump over the mid-20s options; this is the single biggest
  swing for a Combat Rogue between 30 and 45.

### Vanquisher's Sword — level 37, Q, both factions (dungeon quest)
- Razorfen Downs dungeon quest, 30.0 DPS 1H sword. Quest name **disagrees between sources**: WIC
  gives "Bring the End" (A table) and "Bring the Light" (H table); WT gives "Bring the Light" for
  Alliance. Verify on the item's Wowhead page.

### Corruption of Earth and Seed — level 45, Q-dungeon (Maraudon)
- Thrash Blade (35.2 DPS 1H sword) is on the Rogue list. Needs a Maraudon run.

### Azsharite Weaponry — level 55, Q, Stranglethorn Vale
- Enchanted Azsharite Felbane **Sword** (35.8 DPS) or **Dagger** (35.7 DPS). Both factions.

### Blade of Reckoning — level 53, Q (Blackrock Depths)
- "Jail Break!" — 35.8 DPS 1H sword. Both factions.

## Non-weapon and class-quest notes

- **Poisons quest** (L20): A "Mathias and the Defias" (Mathias Shaw, Stormwind) / H "Mission:
  Possible But Not Probable" (Shenthul, Orgrimmar) — unlocks poisons (needs 75 Lockpicking);
  **no gear** (NTB, IV). The Thistle Tea questline is the other class quest in IV.
- **Ravenholdt questline** (L50+): IV lists Whisperwalk Boots and Duskbat Drape as rewards that
  "have their own place in end gear sets", and Ebon Mask (replaced early in endgame). Best
  non-weapon rogue quest reward; timing/location of the chain start was not captured.
- **Lockpicking** (L16+): skill, not gear.
- Dual-wield leveling means the off-hand matters too; the tables above are the main-hand lists and
  the guides don't separately list off-hand upgrades.
