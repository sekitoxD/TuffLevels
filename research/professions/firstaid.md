## Leveling path

**1-125** (needs ~119 Linen Cloth, ~55 Wool Cloth)
- Train Apprentice First Aid.
- Craft Linen Bandage 1-40 (39 Linen Cloth), then train Heavy Linen
  Bandage at 40 and craft it 41-80 (80 more Linen Cloth total across
  that span).
- Train Journeyman First Aid, then Wool Bandage + Anti-Venom.
- Craft Wool Bandage 81-115 (35 Wool Cloth), train Heavy Wool Bandage,
  craft it 116-125 (20 more Wool Cloth).

**125-225** (needs ~50 Wool Cloth, ~90 Silk Cloth, ~15 Mageweave Cloth)
- Purchase *Expert First Aid - Under Wraps*, *Manual: Heavy Silk
  Bandage*, and *Manual: Mageweave Bandage* (Auction House or First Aid
  vendors).
- Learn Expert First Aid from the manual, craft Heavy Wool Bandage
  126-150 (50 Wool Cloth).
- Train Silk Bandage, craft it 151-180 (30 Silk Cloth).
- Learn Heavy Silk Bandage from its manual, craft it 181-210 (60 Silk
  Cloth).
- Learn Mageweave Bandage from its manual, craft it 211-225 (15
  Mageweave Cloth).

**The "Triage" quest** — at character level 35 *and* First Aid skill
225, the breadcrumb quest **Alliance Trauma** / **Horde Trauma**
becomes available, leading to **Triage**. The breadcrumb is optional —
players who already meet the level/skill requirement can travel
straight to Arathi Highlands or Dustwallow Marsh to pick up Triage
directly. Completing Triage is how you learn **Artisan First Aid** —
this is a real quest reward, not a vendor/trainer unlock, so it's a
genuine candidate for a route `note` (or an `optional` step) on any
character passing through Arathi Highlands or Dustwallow Marsh around
level 35+.

**225-300** (needs ~55 Mageweave Cloth, ~50 Runecloth)
- Craft Mageweave Bandage 226-240 (15 more Mageweave Cloth).
- Train Heavy Mageweave Bandage (Doctor Gustaf VanHowzen or Doctor
  Gregory Victor), craft it 241-260 (40 Mageweave Cloth).
- Train Runecloth Bandage, craft it 261-290 (30 Runecloth).
- Train Heavy Runecloth Bandage, craft it 290-300 (20 Runecloth).
- Alternative: with enough spare Mageweave Cloth, Heavy Mageweave
  Bandage alone can carry you to 300.
