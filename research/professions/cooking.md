# Cooking (1-300)

Research aid, not addon content — see `research/README.md` for the
workflow and citation conventions this file follows. No in-addon
tracking exists for professions; this is reference-only, the same as
`research/classes/rogue.md`'s community-sourced sections.

**Source:** the "Cooking" tab of the community
["Rogue Master Sheet - Horde"](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/13MuieeftxH00hJo7njbydnCgHgcMEtt_u3w1tZVU04s)
Google Sheet. No further citation is given on that tab for where its
numbers came from — treat the recipe/tier breakpoints as a reasonable
community-reported starting point, not Wowhead-verified.

## Trainer tiers

| Tier | Skill range | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Apprentice | 1-75 | Cooking trainer |
| Journeyman | 75-150 | Cooking trainer |
| Expert | 150-225 | *Expert Cookbook*, sold by Shandrinal (Silverwind Refuge, Ashenvale, Alliance) or Wulan (Shadowprey Village, Desolace, Horde) for 1 gold |
| Artisan | 225-300 | Reward from the quest **Clamlette Surprise**, offered by Dirge Quikcleave in Gadgetzan, Tanaris — a real quest, not a vendor/trainer unlock, and Horde-friendly location |

## Suggested leveling recipes by bracket

- **1-50**: Charred Wolf Meat / Roasted Boar Meat / Brilliant Smallfish
- **50-75**: Smoked Bear Meat / Longjaw Mud Snapper
- **75-100**: Crab Cake / Cooked Crab Claw / Longjaw Mud Snapper
- **100-150**: Seasoned Wolf Kabob / Curiously Tasty Omelet / Bristle Whisker Catfish
- **150-175**: Curiously Tasty Omelet / Hot Lion Chops / Bristle Whisker Catfish
- **175-200**: Soothing Turtle Bisque / Roast Raptor / Mithril Headed Trout
- **200-225**: Spider Sausage / Roast Raptor / Mithril Headed Trout
- **225-250**: Monster Omelet / Tender Wolf Steak / Spotted Yellowtail
- **250-275**: Monster Omelet / Tender Wolf Steak / Poached Sunscale Salmon
- **275-300**: Smoked Desert Dumplings / Mightfish Steak
