## Recipe order by skill bracket

**1-100**
- 1-30: 60x Rough Blasting Powder (60 Rough Stone). Keep all 60 — you
  need them again later for Rough Copper Bomb.
- 30-50: 30x Handful of Copper Bolts (30 Copper Bar). Keep ~30 of these
  too. Make your Arclight Spanner somewhere in this 50-70 window and
  hold onto it — a later recipe needs it.
- 50-75: 30x Rough Copper Bomb (60 Rough Blasting Powder + 30 Handful
  of Copper Bolts).
- 75-90: 20x Coarse Blasting Powder (20 Coarse Stone). Keep all 20.
- 90-100: 20x Coarse Dynamite (20 Coarse Blasting Powder + 20 Linen
  Cloth).

**100-160**
- 100-113: 13x Clockwork Box (39 Bronze Bar).
- 113-125: 1x Flying Tiger Goggles (8 Bronze Bar + 2 Tiger's Eye), using
  the Clockwork Box you just made.
- 125-130: 30x Heavy Blasting Powder (30 Heavy Stone).
- 130-150: 15x Whirring Bronze Gizmo (30 Bronze Bar + 15 Wool Cloth).
  You'll need 30 Heavy Blasting Powder and 15 Whirring Bronze Gizmo
  again shortly — making these should land you around 150 skill.
- 150-160: 15x Bronze Framework (30 Bronze Bar + 15 Medium Leather + 15
  Wool Cloth). Keep these too, you'll likely overshoot 160 slightly.

**160-216**
- 160-175: 15x Explosive Sheep (30 Heavy Blasting Powder + 15 Whirring
  Bronze Gizmo + 15 Bronze Framework + 30 Wool Cloth). Keep 5 in case
  you go Goblin Engineering at 200.
- 175-176: 1x Gyromatic Micro-Adjustor (4 Steel Bar) — a tool needed for
  later recipes, not consumed for skill-ups past this point.
- 176-195: 60x Solid Blasting Powder (120 Solid Stone). Save these for
  later.
- 195-200: 7x Mithril Tube (21 Mithril Bar). Stop at 200 and keep 6 in
  case you go Gnomish Engineering at 200. If short of 200, a few Iron
  Grenades fill the gap.

**216-300**
- 216-238: 40x Mithril Casing (120 Mithril Bar). You'll need these
  again shortly.
- 238-250: 20x Hi-Explosive Bomb (40 Mithril Casing + 20 Unstable
  Trigger + 40 Solid Blasting Powder). Likely reaches ~255.
- 250-260: 20x Dense Blasting Powder (40 Dense Stone) — may need more
  than 20 to actually reach 260.
- 260-285: 25x Thorium Widget (75 Thorium Bar + 25 Runecloth). This
  recipe turns yellow near the end of its range, so it may not reach
  285 on its own — the next recipe picks up the remainder.
- 285-300: 20x Thorium Tube (120 Thorium Bar). Turns green for the last
  few points, so more than 20 may be needed.
